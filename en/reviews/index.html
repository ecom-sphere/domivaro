Every client authorised us to publish their review with their first name and their town. We publish neither their address nor their photographs. Reviews are dated, and we answer publicly to anyone who asks, including when the review is critical.

How these reviews are checked. Every published review is tied to a signed contract and to at least 1 paid invoice: we only publish clients we have invoiced. We ask all our clients after 3 months, with the same message for everyone, and we do not filter the answers. No review is bought, and none is given in exchange for a discount. Of the 34 owners we look after, 12 agreed to publication.

The original language. 4 reviews were written in English and appear as they came. The other 8, written in Dutch or French, are translated by us; the original language is shown under each one, and the original text is sent to you on request.

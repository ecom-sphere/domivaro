Domivaro/Our area

13 places, and the ones we turn down

We cover the whole of l'Alacantí, the comarca we live in, and the part of the Marina Baixa that stays within 35 minutes by road, as far as Villajoyosa. A comarca boundary, not a stopwatch: the times below are the median of 3 independent route calculators.

Where we go

We cover all of l'Alacantí and the south of the Marina Baixa. We turn down anything too far, because a visit every 15 days does not hold up if the drive is over 35 minutes.

What we turn down. Anything over 35 minutes by road from Busot, starting with the north of our former area: Benidorm at 40 minutes, l'Alfàs del Pi and Finestrat at 41, Sella at 43, La Nucia at 45, Altea at 46. Then Polop, Callosa d'en Sarrià, Guadalest, Confrides, Tàrbena. The whole Marina Alta: Calpe, Benissa, Benitachell, Teulada and Moraira, Dénia, Jávea. And what is close in time but outside our 2 comarcas: Santa Pola, Elx, Alcoi, Ibi, Castalla, Novelda, Petrer, Aspe. Beyond 35 minutes, an isolated house takes us more road time than useful time, and there are 2 of us. The road goes there, our availability does not. If your house is there, tell us: we will give you a name, not a quote.

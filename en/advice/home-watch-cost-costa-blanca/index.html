How much does home watch cost on the Costa Blanca?

Almost nobody in this trade publishes prices. Here are the figures we checked on the Costa Blanca, our own included, and above all what each price range really covers.

Published on 6 August 2026 · A 5 minute read

The word first, the figures after

Home watch is the name this service goes by in the United States, where the trade is 20 years ahead of Europe. In Spain, one legal precision explains part of the price differences you will see below.

Under Spanish law, guarding and protecting property is a reserved activity under law 5/2014, open to licensed security companies only. No property care company can sell you guarding, and Domivaro carries out no activity of that kind. What you actually buy has 4 names: key holding, upkeep, technical checks and a written record of the state of the house. That is the market we price here, with checked and dated figures.

What moves the price: 6 factors

1

The number of visits

From 1 a month for a closed flat to 4 or more for a villa with a pool. It is the first factor, and it should always be written into the contract.

2

Surface and pool

A pool adds checks to every visit, off season included. Above 350 m², expect a tailor-made plan.

3

Labour included, or billed

Tightening a hose, bleeding a tap, changing a seal: included with some providers, billed per call-out with others. That is where the totals drift apart.

4

IVA included, or not

Spanish IVA is 21 %. A price shown before tax is really 21 % higher when you pay. Check the wording before you compare.

5

Insurance with a figure, or without

Many write the word insured, almost nobody writes the amount. Ours: €600,000 per claim, with a €30,000 key-loss extension.

6

Unplanned interventions

A written quote approved before any work, or an invoice discovered afterwards: ask the question before signing, not after the first incident.

5 questions to ask before signing, with us or anyone

Is the price final, with 21 % IVA included?

The right answer is yes, in writing on the quote. A before-tax price looks 21 % cheaper than it is. With us, every published price includes IVA.

How many visits a month, and are they in writing?

The exact number of visits belongs in the contract, not in a spoken promise. It is what determines whether your insurer treats the house as looked after.

Is the insurance a written figure?

Ask for the amount per claim and a current certificate. A seriously insured provider hands them over without hesitation: with us, €600,000 per claim, certificate delivered with the contract.

What happens when a repair goes beyond the plan?

The right answer: a written quote, approved by you before any work, and no invoice without prior agreement. Be wary of plans where every incident opens the door to open-ended billing.

What commitment, and what notice to stop?

Look for a monthly contract with no fixed term, cancellable by a simple message with reasonable notice. With us: 14 calendar days to withdraw, then 30 days notice, with no penalty.

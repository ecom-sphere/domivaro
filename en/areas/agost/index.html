What goes wrong in Agost when the house stays shut

Agost is an inland village known for its terracotta jars. More than 1 home in 3 is not lived in year-round, and the old village is full of terraced houses.

Your keys, and the language

Your key is kept in the sealed key cabinet, sealed again every time it is closed, on a tag carrying a code and never your name or your address. Only Fabrizio and Krystelle have the code.

Krystelle is a native Spanish speaker. The call to the town hall, to the community of owners, to the insurer or to the tradesman is hers to make. You never make a call in Spanish.

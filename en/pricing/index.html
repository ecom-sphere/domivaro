The case where the cheapest costs the most

This is not about quality, it is about deadlines. We opened 17 home insurance contracts sold in Spain. None of them requires a periodic inspection, and we write that even though the opposite would suit us. What 7 of them do require is an action, within a deadline.

The last line is the one that puts a figure on the difference. With Ibex and HDI, 60 consecutive days without anyone you have entrusted with a key calling in, and the house contractually becomes unoccupied. A visit every 8 weeks is 56 days: 4 days of margin on paper. A visit every month resets the counter 30 days before the deadline.

And the action only counts if it can be proved. Articles 11 and 12 of law 50/1980 reduce the indemnity pro rata when an increase in risk has not been declared, and release the insurer entirely in case of bad faith. In front of a loss adjuster, a closed stopcock with no dated photograph is worth nothing. That is what the report sent the same evening is for.

If all you want is someone holding your key, buy key holding: it is cheaper, and it is enough. If you want to be able to prove what was done and when, compare what is included, not the published price.

3 money questions, no dodging

They all come up, in the same order. Here are the answers, with nothing dodged.

Can I stop whenever I want?

Yes, in 2 ways. Within the 14 calendar days following signature you can withdraw without reason or penalty, with a simple message: it is a right, and the model form comes with the contract. After that, the contract is monthly, cancellable with 30 days notice by a simple message. No penalty, no minimum term, no automatic 12 month renewal. We would rather you stayed because the service is useful to you.

Are the published prices the final prices?

Yes. They include tax, with 21% IVA, and cover the number of visits and the labour hours stated. Any work outside the package is quoted in writing and you approve it before anything starts. No invoice is ever issued without your prior agreement.

How do I pay from abroad, do I need a Spanish account?

No. SEPA direct debit from your Dutch, Belgian, British or French account, or a monthly transfer, your choice. Spanish invoice with 21% IVA included, emailed on the 1st of the month. No deposit, no set up fee, no card stored. You stop with a simple message and 30 days notice.

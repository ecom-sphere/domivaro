How we work

The same protocol on every visit, in the same order, whatever the house. That is what makes the reports comparable from one month to the next.

Before

We come in

2 visits a month, on spaced dates not announced in advance. Water and electricity readings, opening up, airing, access check.

During

We check

38 points in a fixed order: visible roof, gutters, terraces, seals, plumbing, water heater, air conditioning, pool, irrigation, breakers, damp, pests, letterbox.

After

We repair, then we write

Small repairs are done on the spot, within the fee. Beyond that, you get a quote with photos before anything is committed. The report goes out within 24 hours.

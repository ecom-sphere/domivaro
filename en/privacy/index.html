Privacy

This policy describes the processing of personal data under European regulation 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spanish organic law 3/2018.

The data controller

DOMIVARO SERVICIOS S.L., CIF B-53123451, Carrer Sant Pere 42, 03590 Altea, Alicante, Spain.

Contact: contact@domivaro.com, or by telephone on +34 611 42 08 73.

Given the size of the business and the nature of the processing, appointing a data protection officer is not required under article 37 of the GDPR. Requests are handled directly by the management.

What we collect and why

Through the contact form: your name, your email address, your telephone if you give it, the town where the property is, and how you describe it. This is used only to reply to you and to prepare the free check visit.

As a client: the data needed to perform the contract and to invoice, together with the visit reports and their photographs.

No data is sold, rented or passed to a third party for commercial purposes. No automated chasing is sent.

Legal basis

For the form: your consent, article 6.1.a. For clients: performance of the contract, article 6.1.b, and Spanish accounting obligations, article 6.1.c.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that go no further are deleted after 12 months. Contract data and reports are kept for the duration of the contract, then 6 years under Spanish accounting and tax obligations.

Recipients and transfers

Data is shared only with the people who come to your house and, where relevant, with the tradesman you approved for a specific repair.

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. The servers used are located in the European Union. No transfer outside the European Union is made for form data.

Accounting is handled by a firm established in Spain, bound by a processor agreement under article 28 of the GDPR.

Requests sent through the form are stored with Supabase, a database host in the European Union region, bound by a processor agreement under article 28 of the GDPR.

Trackers

This site sets no advertising tracker and uses no third party audience measurement. Only a local technical marker may be written in your browser, to remember that you declined the language suggestion. It never leaves your device.

Security

Visit reports and their photographs are stored encrypted. Access is named and limited to the 2 partners.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. Exercise it with a simple message to the address above; the answer comes within one month.

You may also contact the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos, the supervisory authority in Spain.
